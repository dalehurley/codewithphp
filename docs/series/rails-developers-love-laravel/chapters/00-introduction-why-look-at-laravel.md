## Overview

Coming from Ruby on Rails? You're in for a pleasant surprise. Laravel isn't just "PHP's Rails"—it's a framework that learned from Rails, refined its best ideas, and added its own innovations. This chapter explores why Laravel deserves your attention and how your Rails knowledge gives you a head start.

## What You'll Learn

- Why Laravel appeals to Rails developers
- How Laravel's philosophy aligns with Rails
- The key differences and similarities
- What makes Laravel special in the PHP ecosystem

## Laravel's Rails-Inspired Design

Laravel creator Taylor Otwell has openly acknowledged Rails as a major inspiration. You'll find familiar patterns:

- **Convention over configuration** — Like Rails, Laravel makes sensible defaults
- **Elegant, expressive syntax** — Similar philosophy to Ruby's readability
- **Batteries included** — Comprehensive tooling out of the box
- **Developer happiness** — Focus on joy and productivity

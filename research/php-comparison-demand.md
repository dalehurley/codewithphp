## Trends and ordering within bands

Google Trends was checked worldwide over the previous 12 months on 20 November 2026, grouping both word orders and using Go as a common reference in several batches. Python led JavaScript and HTML in their shared batch. Node.js scored above Go in its own batch, and Swift matched Go in another. Trends rescales each batch and may show zero for low-volume phrases.
